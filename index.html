    Hype vs. evidence¶

    “Lion’s Mane regrows your brain.”

    Rated D overall; at most C− for mild cognitive impairment.

    “Dihexa is millions of times stronger than BDNF.”

    No checkable publication supports this claim.

    “Bacopa works right away.”

    Rated B for memory, but only after at least 12 weeks.

    “Creatine is just for the gym.”

    Rated B for vegetarians, older adults and sleep-deprived people.

    “Caffeine is too basic.”

    One of only three A ratings in the course: vigilance and attention, especially under fatigue.

    Questions¶

    Is this medical advice?

    No. The course describes research evidence. Study doses are reported so the evidence can be judged, not as a recommendation. Several substances are prescription-only, controlled or have never been tested for safety in humans.

    How do I get access after buying?

    You receive a password that unlocks the full course on this website, in any browser. The PDF and offline version are included.

    Do I need a science background?

    It helps, but Module 1 covers the foundations: neurotransmitters, plasticity, energy metabolism and the blood–brain barrier. Technical terms are explained in the glossary.

    Does the course tell me what to take?

    It shows what was studied, at which doses, for whom, and how good the evidence is. What you do with that is your decision, ideally together with a doctor.

    Who wrote it?

    Written by Yulbig and published by Nootropedia. Every named study was checked against PubMed, the publisher or an official source. See About and the references.
